How it works

1
Watch proven wallets. Polymarket's public leaderboards across all-time, monthly and weekly windows define who counts as smart money. Their large buys come off the public trade feed.
2
Throw most of it away. Twelve filters reject in-play markets, anything resolving too soon or too far out, stale signals, prices that already ran past the whale's entry, and duplicate bets on one event.
3
Rank and size what's left. Conviction blends wallet agreement, size, freshness, price drift and time remaining. Position size respects the house per-market caps against your current capital.
4
You place it. Nothing here executes a trade. The board's only job is to bring you a short, honest list.
Advisory only. This project has no execution path — nothing here places, cancels, or manages an order. Every trade is placed by hand; the code that once placed orders is disabled at the constructor.
An empty board is a normal and usually correct outcome — most days nothing clears the filters. Capital and journal are stored only in this browser and are never published.
